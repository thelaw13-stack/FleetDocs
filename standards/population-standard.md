# Fleet Population Standard

Version 1.0

## Core rules

1. Populate only; do not redesign the template.
2. One asset equals one complete, continuously copyable Build Sheet.
3. Optimize for a roughly 30-second read.
4. Separate verified fact, estimate, and unknown. Preserve provenance inline where qualification matters.
5. Preferred evidence order: measurement or physical inspection; invoice or receipt; manufacturer documentation; photo verification; owner record; seller statement.
6. Configuration describes only what is installed today. Do not include installation stories, chronology, reasoning, or costs there.
7. History on the Build Sheet is only a brief index. Full chronology belongs in Vehicle History.
8. Ownership Costs are summaries. Line-item purchases and receipts belong in the Parts Catalog or Vehicle History.
9. Every line must earn its place. Remove duplication, marketing language, repeated descriptions, and long explanations.
10. Build Sheet records current system-level configuration; the Parts Catalog records name, brand, part number, date, vendor, cost, source, and verification status.

## Missing and uncertain data

- `UNKNOWN`: fact is not known.
- `TBD`: a future decision or value is intentionally undecided.
- `N/A`: field does not apply.
- `ESTIMATED` or `~`: approximate amount or value.
- Qualify recollections, e.g. `1835cc (Owner Record — Pending Verification)`.

Never replace a missing fact with a plausible value.

## Cost discipline

Keep original purchase, transfer/acquisition value, owner-funded upgrades, maintenance, and insurance repairs separate. Do not count an insurance-funded repair as owner investment. Do not allocate a bundled invoice to individual parts without evidence.
